Antalya Airport to Lara transfer

Lara and Kundu are among the closest resort areas to Antalya Airport. A private AYT Ride transfer is a simple choice for families, couples and business visitors who want a direct hotel pickup without waiting for a shared shuttle.

Why book this route in advance?

Your route, flight number, luggage and pickup time are sent by WhatsApp before arrival. AYT Ride confirms the driver plan and the final price before the ride is locked in.

Payment

No online payment is collected in this first phase. Guests pay in cash after the transfer is completed.
