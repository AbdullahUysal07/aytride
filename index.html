Before you book

Questions Antalya visitors ask before choosing a transfer.

How does the booking work?

Fill the route, flight and passenger details, then send the prepared WhatsApp and email request. AYT Ride confirms the vehicle and pickup point before the booking is final.

Do I pay online?

No. In the first phase, guests pay in cash after the ride is completed. This is shown clearly before the request is sent.

Which Antalya routes are covered?

Airport, hotel, VIP, city and golf transfers are available for Lara, Kundu, Kaleici, Konyaalti, Belek, Kadriye, Kemer, Side, Manavgat, Alanya, Kas and Kalkan.

Can I bring golf bags or extra luggage?

Yes. Add luggage and notes in the request. The VIP van is shown when the passenger or suitcase count needs extra space.
