Private ride vs taxi

When private transfer makes more sense in Antalya

A taxi can be fine for short city trips. For airport arrivals, resort routes and groups, a private transfer often gives a clearer experience because the vehicle, route and pickup details are agreed before you travel.

Choose private transfer when

  • You arrive late at night or early in the morning.
  • You travel with family, children, golf bags or many suitcases.
  • Your hotel is outside the city, such as Belek, Kemer, Side or Alanya.
  • You prefer a fixed estimate before the ride begins.

Why WhatsApp helps

With AYT Ride, your route, flight number and passenger details are sent by WhatsApp, so the operator can confirm the exact pickup plan quickly.
