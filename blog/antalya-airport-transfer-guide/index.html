Antalya Airport Transfer Guide 2026: Routes, Prices and Pickup Planning

Arriving at Antalya Airport (AYT) is the starting point for holidays across a long stretch of the Turkish Mediterranean coast. The best transfer choice depends on where you are staying, how much luggage you have and whether you want a direct private ride or a shared journey with additional stops. This guide explains the main AYT transfer routes and what to prepare before you land.

Antalya Airport to Lara and Kundu

Lara and Kundu are among the closest major hotel areas to AYT. A direct private transfer is useful after a late flight or when travelling with children and luggage because your group goes straight to the hotel without shared-shuttle stops. See the Antalya Airport to Lara transfer page for the current vehicle options and booking form.

Antalya Airport to Belek and Kadriye

Belek and Kadriye are popular for resort holidays and golf trips. If you are carrying golf bags, include them in the booking notes so the vehicle choice can be checked before pickup. For route details, use the Antalya Airport to Belek transfer page.

Antalya Airport to Kemer

Kemer lies west of Antalya and the wider resort area includes Beldibi, Goynuk, Kiris, Camyuva and Tekirova. The exact hotel area matters because destinations are spread along the coast. Add your full hotel or accommodation name when requesting an Antalya Airport to Kemer transfer.

Antalya Airport to Side and Manavgat

Side has several resort zones including Kumkoy, Evrenseki, Colakli and Sorgun. Providing the exact hotel makes pickup and journey planning clearer. The Antalya Airport to Side transfer page covers the Side / Manavgat route group and shows the current fixed vehicle prices.

Antalya Airport to Alanya

Alanya is one of the longest high-demand resort transfers from AYT. Hotels and apartments can be in Okurcalar, Avsallar, Turkler, Konakli, central Alanya or Mahmutlar, so the exact destination is important. Check the Antalya Airport to Alanya transfer page before booking. For a long route like Alanya, confirming the vehicle, luggage space, flight details and total price in advance makes the arrival plan much clearer.

What information should you prepare before landing?

Keep your flight number, arrival date and time, hotel or accommodation name, passenger count, luggage count and reachable WhatsApp number ready. If you need child seats or carry oversized items such as golf bags, add them to the request. These details help the transfer team confirm the correct vehicle and meeting plan.

How does Antalya Airport pickup work?

When you submit an AYT Ride request, include your flight number so the pickup can be coordinated around the arrival. The exact meeting point and driver arrangement are confirmed before the booking is final. Keep WhatsApp available after landing in case the meeting instructions need to be checked.

Private transfer or taxi?

A private transfer is planned before arrival and gives you a confirmed route, vehicle and total price. A taxi may be more spontaneous when you leave the terminal. Families, groups and travellers going to longer-distance resorts may prefer having the vehicle and luggage plan arranged in advance. For a fuller comparison, read private transfer vs taxi in Antalya.

Payment and return transfers

AYT Ride's current booking flow does not require online card payment. Payment is made after the ride unless another arrangement is confirmed. If you already know your departure date, you can also request the return journey when making the first booking so both airport transfers are planned together.

Quick booking checklist

Choose your destination, check the displayed fixed price, enter the pickup date and time, add the flight number and hotel, confirm passenger and luggage counts, select the vehicle, then provide your contact details. Review the booking summary before sending the request.
