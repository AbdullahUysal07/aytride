Airport transfer guide

Antalya Airport to Lara, Belek, Side and Alanya

Antalya Airport, known by the airport code AYT, serves many resort areas along the coast. A private transfer is usually the simplest option when you arrive with luggage, children, golf bags or a late flight.

Typical route times

Lara and Kundu are usually the fastest hotel zones from AYT. Belek and Kadriye are popular with resort and golf guests. Side, Manavgat and Alanya need more driving time, so a pre-arranged pickup keeps the arrival calmer.

What to share before pickup

  • Flight number, arrival date and landing time.
  • Hotel, villa, resort or apartment name.
  • Passenger count, suitcases and child seat needs.
  • WhatsApp number for meeting-point updates.

Payment

AYT Ride confirms the pickup point and final price before the booking is final. For the first phase, payment is collected in cash after the ride is completed.
