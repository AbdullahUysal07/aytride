Kemer route guide

Antalya Airport to Kemer: distance and transfer time

The route from Antalya Airport (AYT) to Kemer is approximately 58 km. A private transfer is planned at around 65 minutes, although traffic and the exact hotel location can change the journey time.

How far is Kemer from Antalya Airport?

Kemer lies west of Antalya on the Mediterranean coast. The airport transfer follows the coastal direction toward Kemer, making the exact resort or hotel area important when planning pickup and arrival time.

Antalya Airport to Kemer private transfer

AYT Ride offers a Standard Sedan and VIP Van option for this route. The current route page shows a fixed total of €60 for Standard Sedan and €80 for VIP Van. Vehicle choice should also consider passenger and luggage count.

Arrival and return transfer planning

For airport arrivals, add your flight number and arrival date. For the return journey from Kemer to Antalya Airport, allow enough time for traffic, airport check-in and security procedures.

Private transfer or shared shuttle?

A private transfer goes directly to the booked destination without waiting for other passengers to be dropped at several hotels. This can be useful for families, groups and travelers carrying extra luggage.
