Antalya Airport to Alanya: Distance, Transfer Time and Hotel Areas

The journey from Antalya Airport (AYT) to central Alanya is approximately 125 km and a private transfer normally takes around 120 minutes. The exact journey time depends on traffic and your hotel area, because the Alanya resort corridor extends well beyond the city centre.

Which Alanya hotel area are you travelling to?

“Alanya” can mean several different resort zones along the coast. A booking may be for Okurcalar, Avsallar, Turkler, Konakli, central Alanya or Mahmutlar. Enter the exact hotel, villa or apartment address so the pickup plan and destination are clear before the journey.

Why can Alanya transfer times vary?

The 120-minute figure is an approximate planning time for the main Antalya Airport–Alanya route. Traffic, the time of day and the exact accommodation location can change the journey. Resorts on the western side of the Alanya corridor may be reached earlier than addresses farther east.

Antalya Airport or Gazipasa Airport?

Check the airport code on your flight before arranging transport. Antalya Airport uses the code AYT. Alanya is also served by flights using Gazipasa-Alanya Airport, so confirming the airport code prevents a transfer being arranged from the wrong airport.

What should you send with an Alanya transfer request?

Prepare your flight number, arrival date and time, exact hotel or address, passenger count, luggage count and reachable WhatsApp number. If you need a child seat or carry larger items, include that information before the vehicle is confirmed.

Which vehicle should you choose?

The AYT Ride Standard Sedan is intended for up to 3 passengers and 3 suitcases. The VIP Van fits up to 6 passengers and 6 suitcases and can be more practical for families, groups or travellers carrying additional luggage. Vehicle availability is confirmed with the booking request.

Can you book Alanya to Antalya Airport as a return?

Yes. The booking form supports a return journey. If your departure flight is already known, add the return date and time when making the first request so both directions can be planned together.

How payment works

The current AYT Ride booking flow does not require online card payment. The displayed route price is per selected vehicle, and payment is made after the ride unless another arrangement is confirmed.
