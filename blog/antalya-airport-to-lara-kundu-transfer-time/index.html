Antalya Airport to Lara and Kundu: distance and transfer time

Lara and Kundu are among the closest major hotel areas to Antalya Airport (AYT). The route is approximately 14 km with an estimated private transfer time of around 20 minutes, depending on traffic and the exact hotel entrance.
