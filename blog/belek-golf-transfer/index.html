Belek and Kadriye are approximately 33 km from Antalya Airport (AYT), with a private transfer normally taking around 35 minutes. The area is popular with resort guests and golf groups, so hotel gates and luggage requirements are worth confirming before pickup.

Add golf luggage before the vehicle is confirmed

Golf bags take more space than standard suitcases and can change the most suitable vehicle. Add golf bags in the booking notes. For a golf group or several large bags, a VIP Van can be more practical than a sedan.

Belek and Kadriye hotel gates

Large resorts can have separate security gates, lobbies and accommodation blocks. Enter the exact hotel name and, when known, the lobby or block so the meeting point can be confirmed clearly.

What should you send with the transfer request?

Prepare your flight number, arrival date and time, hotel name, passenger count, suitcase count, golf-bag details and reachable WhatsApp number. Add child-seat requirements before vehicle confirmation.

Return transfer from Belek to Antalya Airport

If the departure flight is already known, book the return direction at the same time. Pickup planning should allow for hotel departure, road traffic and airport procedures.
