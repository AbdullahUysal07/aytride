Belek and Kadriye golf transfer checklist

Belek and Kadriye are among the most popular Antalya golf areas. A private van is a practical choice when your group carries golf bags, suitcases and resort equipment.

Before you book

  • Count golf bags separately from suitcases.
  • Share the exact resort or villa name.
  • Tell the operator if you need a return airport transfer.
  • Use WhatsApp for arrival-gate and pickup updates.

Vehicle choice

A VIP van or Executive XL is usually more comfortable for golf groups than a standard sedan. The AYT Ride form shows larger vehicles when passenger or luggage counts increase.
