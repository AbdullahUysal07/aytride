Antalya Airport to Side: Distance, Transfer Time and Resort Areas

The journey from Antalya Airport (AYT) to Side is approximately 65 km and a private transfer normally takes around 60 minutes. Your exact travel time can vary with traffic and the location of your hotel in the wider Side and Manavgat resort area.

Which Side resort area are you travelling to?

Hotels marketed as Side may be located in Kumkoy, Evrenseki, Colakli, Sorgun or around Manavgat. Enter the exact hotel or accommodation name when booking so the destination and pickup plan can be confirmed correctly.

Why can Side transfer times vary?

The 60-minute figure is an approximate planning time. Traffic, arrival time and the exact resort zone can change the journey. Hotels farther from the main airport route may require additional local driving.

What details should you prepare?

Have your flight number, arrival date and time, hotel name, passenger count, luggage count and reachable WhatsApp number ready. Add child-seat requests or oversized luggage before the vehicle is confirmed.

Which vehicle is suitable?

The AYT Ride Standard Sedan is intended for up to 3 passengers and 3 suitcases. The VIP Van fits up to 6 passengers and 6 suitcases and can be more practical for families and groups.

Can you book Side to Antalya Airport as a return?

Yes. The booking form supports return journeys. If your departure flight is known, add the return date and time with the first request so both directions can be planned together.
